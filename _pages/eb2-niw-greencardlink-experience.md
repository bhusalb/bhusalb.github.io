_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

This page describes my own experience as a client, based on my signed retainer agreement, written correspondence, and documents in my possession. Statements of opinion are identified as my opinion.

## Hiring the firm

In November 2024, I hired Aria Vatankhah of Green Card Link (Palo Alto, California) to prepare and file my EB-2 National Interest Waiver (NIW) Form I-140 petition. Under our signed retainer agreement, I paid a flat attorney fee of $6,000.

The agreement states that if the I-140 petition is denied, the attorney "will refund 100% of the 'attorney fees' submitted." The firm's website describes the same policy as an "Approval or No Fee" service.

## The refund term

In February and March 2026, while my petition was pending, the firm emailed me that, because of changes at USCIS, it "would no longer be fair to hold either of us to that original refund term," and offered a free refiling instead of a refund. I did not agree to change the agreement. In May 2026, the firm confirmed in writing: "we will fully refund your attorney fees if the case is denied, as stated in our contract."

## The RFE response brief

After USCIS issued a Request for Evidence, the firm shared an "RFE Response Brief" with me on August 4, 2026.

On November 5, 2026, I ran that document through Pangram (version 4.1), a commercial AI-detection tool. **Pangram's report rated the 9,451-word brief 100% AI generated** (0.1% AI assisted, 0% human written), with the summary: "We believe that this entire text is AI."

I am reporting the result the tool gave me. Given the attorney fees I paid, the result concerned me.

## Denial and refund

USCIS denied my petition on September 21, 2026, and I requested my refund that day.

The firm has acknowledged in writing that it is "not disputing our obligation to refund the attorney fees." It has said it is not able to refund the full amount in a single payment and has proposed monthly installments of $200, and later $300. The agreement does not provide for installments, and I have not agreed to them.

As of November 6, 2026, I have not received any refund payment.

## Where things stand

I have filed a complaint with the State Bar of California and am pursuing fee arbitration. I will update this page if the matter is resolved or if any of the facts above change.

## My opinion

Based on my experience, I would not recommend this firm to EB-2 NIW applicants without first reading the refund terms carefully and asking how and when refunds are actually paid.
